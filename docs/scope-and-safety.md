# Scope and safety

## Included

Encounter detection, relative motion, risk scoring, alerting, near-miss mining, explainability and
benchmarking.

## Excluded

Fairway design, waterway geometry, route optimization, waypoint generation, autonomous manoeuvre
selection and navigational infrastructure design.

!!! warning
    PortGuard-AIS is not certified bridge equipment and must not be used as the sole source of a
    navigational decision.
